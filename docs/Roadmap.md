## Principles

RepoPulse prioritizes:

1. Useful engineering insights over vanity metrics.
2. Correctness over unnecessary features.
3. Simple architecture before premature scaling.
4. Security and privacy by default.
5. Open-source contribution and transparency.

The roadmap may change as the product and its requirements evolve.
